The Role

What is an AI Creative Producer?

As an AI Creative Producer, Ingrid Marco bridges the gap between technical prompt engineering and high-end aesthetic direction. Niara Studio was born from the belief that AI isn't just a tool, but a canvas for a new era of expression.

We specialize in bespoke workflows that transform abstract concepts into cinematic realities. Ingrid's role is to curate the infinite possibilities of generative technology into a cohesive, brand-aligned narrative, ensuring that every frame and every pixel resonates with intentional human artistry.
